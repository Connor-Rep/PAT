PAT Testing Dundee

SAFETAY Electrical Testing specialise in Portable Appliance Testing (PAT Testing) in Dundee, Perth, Fife & Aberdeen. All our Engineers are City & Guilds qualified and we provide portable appliance testing for any size of business at your convenience ensuring minimal disruption to staff and customers.

We can provide PAT Testing 24 hours a day 7 days a week so work can be carried out at a time that suits you.

PAT Testing is an essential part of your workplaces health and safety programme. The health and Safety Executive report that 25% of all accidents in the workplace involve portable appliances and you have a legal obligation as an employer and an employee under the Electricity At Work Regulations to ensure all appliances are safe to use.

Benefits Of PAT Testing in the workplace

  • Improved Safety for employees and the general public
  • Ensures compliance with your insurance company
  • Reduced insurance costs
  • Increased productivity through safe and reliable appliances
  • Extra peace of mind for you!
  • Conform with the latest Health & Safety Regulations
  • Minimise the risk of fire or injury from faulty electrical appliances
