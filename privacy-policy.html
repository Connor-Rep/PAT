Privacy Policy

Your data, in plain English

This page explains what personal data we collect through this website, why, and what rights you have over it. Last updated 27 August 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Safetay Electrical Testing.

  • Address: Unit 12, The Old Dairy, Meikleour, Perthshire, PH2 6FB
  • Phone: 07775 435 872
  • Email: info@safetaypat.co.uk

We're a small business, so we don't have a dedicated data protection officer. If you have any question about your data or this policy, the email address above goes straight to the person who handles it.

What data we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us. If you use the Get a Quote form, that can include:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Business or property name (optional)
  • Roughly how many appliances need testing
  • Whatever details you write in the message box

We don't take payment details through this website, we don't use analytics or tracking scripts, and we don't set cookies. If you just browse the site without submitting the form, we don't collect anything about you at all beyond the standard access logs any web host keeps (see "Hosting" below).

How the enquiry form actually works

The Get a Quote form doesn't post your details to a server or database we run. Pressing "Send Enquiry" opens your own email app with a pre-filled message, addressed to us, that you then choose to send. That means your details travel from your device to our inbox the same way any email does, and this website itself never stores or sees your submission.

Why we collect it, and our lawful basis

We use the information you send us to respond to your enquiry, provide a quote, and arrange PAT/EET testing if you go ahead. Under UK GDPR, our lawful basis for this is:

  • Your consent — you choose to fill in and send the form.
  • Taking steps towards a contract — handling a quote request you've asked for, ahead of any booking.

We don't use your details for marketing, and we don't add you to any mailing list.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or share your personal data with third parties for their own use. The only outside party involved at all is our web host:

  • Hosting: this website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., part of Microsoft). GitHub serves the site's files and, like any web host, keeps standard server access logs (such as IP addresses of visitors). We don't have a way to link that to who you are, and it isn't linked to anything you submit through the form, since form submissions never touch our hosting, only your own email.

We don't use any analytics, advertising, CRM, or email marketing tools on this site.

International transfers

GitHub's hosting infrastructure may be located outside the UK and European Economic Area (EEA), for example in the United States. This only affects the website's own files (text, images, code), not your personal data, since enquiry details go directly from your email app to ours and never pass through GitHub's servers. Where GitHub does process any data as part of hosting (such as server logs), it does so under its own standard contractual safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with your enquiry and any testing work that follows from it, and delete them once they're no longer needed. If you'd like us to delete an enquiry sooner, just ask using the contact details above.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you
  • Correct anything that's inaccurate
  • Delete your data
  • Restrict or object to how we use it

To exercise any of these, email info@safetaypat.co.uk. If you're not happy with how we've handled your data, you can also complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies or any similar tracking technology.

Changes to this policy

If we ever start using cookies, analytics, or any other tool that changes what data we collect, we'll update this page and change the date at the top.
